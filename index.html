How the score works
  • Each factor is scored 0–1. The score is the slider-weighted average, shown out of 100.
  • Food quality blends food rating, vendor rating and share of 5-star vs 1-star reviews. Ratings with few reviews are pulled toward the city average, and missing ratings count as average instead of zero.
  • Closeness uses real distance and halves every 2 km.
  • Low price, popularity and quality are ranked against all restaurants, so one extreme outlier can't squash everyone else.
